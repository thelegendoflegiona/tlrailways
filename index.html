The LoL–Spawn
Railway Link.

The TLSRL is The LoL's most ambitious infrastructure engineering achievement. Currently featuring 3,066 blocks of precision-measured track from the eastern start to The LoL Valley Station, with planned extensions on both ends.

The route traverses 11 bridges, 2 tunnels, and 7 distinct biomes with full passenger and cargo amenities. Phase 1 (1,892 blocks) terminates at Malacca Station; Phase 2 (1,174 blocks) continues to The LoL Valley.
